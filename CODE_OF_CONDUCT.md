# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
**Positive behavior:** welcoming language, respecting viewpoints, accepting criticism, focusing on community.
**Unacceptable:** trolling, insults, harassment, publishing private information.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
